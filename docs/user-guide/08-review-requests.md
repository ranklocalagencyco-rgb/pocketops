# 8 · Review requests

Reviews win local work, and the best moment to ask is right after the
customer has paid. This module asks for you, every time, automatically.

## How it works

When a job is paid — by card through their link or by **Mark paid** — the
customer gets one text with your review link. No spreadsheet of "people to
ask", no forgetting.

## Setting it up

**Settings → Reviews**:

- **Review link** — usually your Google review link (from your Google
  Business Profile), but any link works.
- **Message** — the wording. `{name}` becomes the customer's first name,
  `{link}` becomes your review link.

## Good to know

- One request per job — a returning customer isn't pestered per visit twice.
- Requests respect your **quiet hours** (Settings → Messaging): a job paid
  at 11pm says thank you at a civilised hour instead.
- It needs your business number connected; until then, paid jobs simply
  don't send anything.
