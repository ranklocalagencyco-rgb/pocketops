# Pocket Ops — User Guide

Short, plain-English guides to the nine core modules. Written for the person
running the business, not the person who built the software.

| # | Guide | What it covers |
|---|-------|----------------|
| 1 | [Your workspace](01-workspace.md) | Signing up and how your company's data is kept private |
| 2 | [Logins, roles & security](02-accounts-and-roles.md) | Team accounts, what each role sees, password resets |
| 3 | [Installing the app](03-install-the-app.md) | Putting Pocket Ops on your phone's home screen |
| 4 | [Jobs from lead to paid](04-job-pipeline.md) | The pipeline every job moves through |
| 5 | [Sign on glass](05-sign-on-glass.md) | Capturing the customer's signature on site |
| 6 | [The customer payment link](06-customer-payment-link.md) | Quotes, deposits, and card payments — no customer account needed |
| 7 | [Missed-call text-back](07-missed-call-text-back.md) | Turning missed calls into leads automatically |
| 8 | [Review requests](08-review-requests.md) | The text that goes out after payment |
| 9 | [QuickBooks sync](09-quickbooks-sync.md) | Paid invoices pushed to your books automatically |

or open **Settings → Help & guides**.
