# 3 · Installing the app

Pocket Ops is a web app that installs like a normal app — no app store, no
updates to approve. Once installed it opens full-screen from its own icon.

## iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Open your Pocket Ops address in Safari and log in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open your Pocket Ops address in Chrome and log in.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu → **Add to Home screen** (or accept the install banner).

## Good to know

- The shield icon on your home screen opens straight to your dashboard.
- Updates arrive automatically the next time you open the app with signal.
- Pages you've visited recently still open with patchy signal, but live
  data (new jobs, messages) needs a connection.
