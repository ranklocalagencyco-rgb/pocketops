# 1 · Your workspace

When you sign up, Pocket Ops creates a **workspace** for your business — your
customers, jobs, invoices, messages, and settings all live inside it.

## What that means in practice

- Everything you see in the app belongs to your business and nobody else's.
  Workspaces are completely separate: another company using Pocket Ops can
  never see your customers, prices, or money.
- Your business details (name, address, logo, VAT number) are set once in
  **Settings → Business** and **Settings → Regional & VAT**, and then appear
  automatically on every quote, invoice, and customer page.
- UK defaults are set up for you at signup: pounds sterling, London time,
  and 20% VAT. Change any of them in **Settings → Regional & VAT**.

## Your public pages

Two pages from your workspace face the outside world:

- **Booking page** — a simple "request a quote" form you can link from your
  website or Google Business Profile. Find the link in **Settings → Business**.
- **Customer links** — each quote/invoice and each customer gets a private,
  unguessable link. Only the person you send it to can open it.
