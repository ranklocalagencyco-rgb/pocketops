# 5 · Sign on glass

Get the customer's signature on the job before you leave site — it settles
"I never agreed to that" disputes before they start.

## How to capture one

1. Open the job on your phone.
2. Tap **Capture signature**, type the customer's name, and hand them the
   phone.
3. They sign with a finger, tap save, and hand it back.

## Where it goes

The signature is stored against the job with the signer's name and the exact
date and time — and the customer can see it on their own quote/invoice link,
so both sides are looking at the same record.

## When to use it

- Accepting a quote in person (instead of through the link)
- Confirming completed work before you invoice
- Any variation agreed on site ("while you're here, could you also…")
