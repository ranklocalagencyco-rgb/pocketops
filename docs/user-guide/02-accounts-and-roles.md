# 2 · Logins, roles & security

Everyone on your team gets their own login — never share passwords.

## Roles

Set each person's role in **Settings → Team**:

| Role | What they see |
|------|---------------|
| **Owner / Admin** | Everything — money, settings, all jobs |
| **Technician / Apprentice** | Only jobs assigned to them, with **no costs or margins** — prices, profit, and hourly rates are hidden |

That masking is done on the server, so a curious tech can't "view source"
their way to your numbers.

## Passwords

- Forgot yours? Use **Forgot password** on the login page — you'll get an
  email link that works once and expires after an hour.
- Every user can update their own name, mobile, and profile photo in
  **Settings → My profile**.

## Sessions

You stay signed in on your device until you log out (the button is next to
your name). If you lose a phone, change your password — that's the key to
the account, not the device.
