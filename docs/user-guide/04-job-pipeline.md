# 4 · Jobs from lead to paid

Every piece of work is a **job**, and every job moves through the same
pipeline — the app only ever offers you the moves that make sense:

```
Lead → Quoted → Approved → Scheduled → In Progress → Completed → Invoiced → Paid
```

A job can be **cancelled** at most stages (you'll be asked why), and a
cancelled job can be reopened as a lead.

## Stage by stage

- **Lead** — an enquiry. Comes in by hand, from your booking page, or from a
  missed-call text-back conversation.
- **Quoted** — you've added line items (labour, materials, fees — use the
  Price Book for one-tap adds) and sent the customer their link. VAT is added
  automatically at your rate.
- **Approved** — the customer accepted, either on their link (automatic) or
  verbally (you move it yourself). If you take deposits, acceptance and the
  deposit payment happen together.
- **Scheduled** — it has a date and time, and shows on the Schedule diary.
- **In Progress → Completed** — on the day. Take photos, log time and
  expenses, capture a signature, issue certificates.
- **Invoiced** — the customer's link becomes a payable invoice. Overdue
  invoices are chased automatically if you've turned that on
  (**Settings → Automations**).
- **Paid** — by card through the link, or **Mark paid** for cash/transfer.
  Either way the review request and QuickBooks sync fire automatically.

## Working under CIS?

Toggle **CIS reverse charge** on the job: VAT is zeroed with the correct
note on the invoice, and the 20% deduction is calculated on labour only.
