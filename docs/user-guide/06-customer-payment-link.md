# 6 · The customer payment link

Every job has one private link that does the whole customer journey: view
the quote → accept it → pay a deposit → view the invoice → pay the balance.
The customer never creates an account or remembers a password.

## What the customer sees

A clean, light page with your logo and business details: the itemised
quote or invoice with VAT (and your VAT number), any deposit already paid,
payments received so far, signatures on file, your credentials (documents
you've chosen to show), and — when there's a balance — a **Pay** button.

## Payments

- Card, Apple Pay, and Google Pay, secured by Stripe. The money settles
  **directly into your bank account** — Pocket Ops never holds your money.
- **Deposits**: set a percentage in **Settings → Regional & VAT** and the
  "Accept quote" button becomes "Accept & pay deposit".
- **Part-payments** are fine — the job only flips to Paid when the balance
  is fully covered.
- Until you've connected Stripe (**Settings → Payments**), the link shows
  your contact details for payment instead of a card button.

## Sending it

Open the job and copy the customer link — text or email it. Each customer
also has a **hub link** showing all their jobs, invoices, and certificates
in one place.
