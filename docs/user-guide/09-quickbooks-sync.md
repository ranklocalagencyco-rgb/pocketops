# 9 · QuickBooks sync

Stop retyping invoices into your accounts. Once connected, every paid
invoice is pushed to QuickBooks automatically.

## Connecting

1. Go to **Settings → Accounting Sync** and choose **Connect QuickBooks**.
2. Log in to QuickBooks and approve the connection (one screen, one click).
3. That's it — the page shows *"Connected — paid invoices sync
   automatically."*

## What syncs

When a job is marked paid, the invoice — customer, line items, VAT, total —
is created in QuickBooks. The **sync history** panel on the same settings
page shows exactly what went across and when, so month-end is a check, not
a chore.

## Good to know

- Sync happens at the moment of payment; there's nothing to run manually.
- If a sync fails (say QuickBooks was disconnected), the history panel shows
  it — reconnect and the records catch up.
- You can disconnect any time with the **Disconnect** button; Pocket Ops
  drops its access immediately and keeps your invoice data untouched.
