# 7 · Missed-call text-back

You're up a ladder, the phone rings out, and that customer calls the next
number on the list. This module texts them back before they do.

## How it works

1. Someone calls your business number and you can't answer.
2. Within seconds they get your missed-call text — something like
   *"Sorry we missed you! Reply here and we'll get straight back to you."*
3. Their reply lands in your **Inbox** as a conversation, so the lead is
   captured even if you can't talk until tonight.

## Setting it up

- **Settings → Messaging** — your business number, the "ring through to"
  number (your handset), and the wording of the text. Use `{name}` and it
  fills in the customer's name when known.
- Calls ring your handset first; the text only goes out if the call isn't
  answered.

## Good to know

- Replies to the text-back are normal SMS conversations — answer them from
  the Inbox on your own schedule.
- Your quiet hours don't block this text: someone who just rang you expects
  an immediate response.
