The dashboard is your morning briefing — open the app and you should know in ten
seconds what today looks like and where the money is.

## The stat cards

The four cards at the top are live totals for your business: work in the pipeline,
what's been invoiced, and what's actually been paid. They update the moment a job
changes stage, so if you've just marked something paid you'll see it here.

## Pipeline bars

Each bar is one stage of the job pipeline (Lead through to Paid) with a count of
jobs sitting in it. A healthy pipeline has jobs spread across stages — a big pile
stuck in **Quoted** usually means quotes need chasing.

If you see a **"going cold"** count, those are leads or quotes that haven't moved
in over 5 days. They also get an amber dot (5+ days) or red dot (10+ days) in the
jobs list, so you know who to ring first.

## Today panel

Jobs scheduled for today, in your timezone. Tap one to open it — from the job page
you can send an "on my way" text or get directions.

## Where jobs come from / why they cancel

Owners and admins also see two insight cards: which sources bring in work (revenue
counts **paid jobs only**, so it reflects real money, not hopeful quotes) and the
reasons jobs get cancelled. Set your list of sources and cancel reasons in
**Settings → Pipeline insights** — the more consistently you tag jobs, the more
useful these get.
