Every piece of work is a **job**, and every job moves through the same pipeline:

**Lead → Quoted → Approved → Scheduled → In Progress → Completed → Invoiced → Paid**

A job can be cancelled at most stages (you'll be asked why — that feeds the
dashboard insights), and a cancelled job can be reopened as a lead.

## Building the quote

Add line items to the job — labour, materials, or fees. Anything you use often
belongs in **Settings → Price Book** so it's a one-tap add. VAT is applied
automatically from your rate in **Settings → Regional & VAT**, and totals
recalculate as you go.

## Sending it to the customer

Move the job to **Quoted** and share the customer's secure link — they can view
the quote, accept it, and pay a deposit online if you've set one. When they
accept, the job moves to **Approved** on its own. You can also download a
quote or invoice PDF, on your own letterhead, any time.

## Doing the work

Schedule it, then move it to **In Progress** on the day. From the job page you
can take photos, log expenses and time, capture a signature on the customer's
screen, and issue certificates (like a CP12 or EICR) where the work needs one.

## Getting paid

Move the job to **Invoiced** and the customer's link becomes a payable invoice —
card payments land straight in your bank via Stripe, and part-payments are fine
(the job only flips to **Paid** when it's fully covered). Cash or bank transfer?
Use **Mark paid** on the job. Either way, the review request text and your
accounting sync happen automatically.

Working under **CIS**? Toggle "CIS reverse charge" on the job — VAT is zeroed
with the proper note on the invoice, and the 20% deduction is calculated on
labour only.
