Every job hangs off a customer record — name, phone, email, and address, with
their full job history underneath.

## Adding customers

Add them by hand, or let them add themselves: enquiries through your public
**booking page** (link in **Settings → Business**) create the customer and a new
lead automatically. Moving your books over from Jobber, Tradify, or ServiceM8?
**Settings → Import & export** takes their CSV files and de-duplicates as it goes.

## The customer hub link

Each customer has a private **hub link** you can text or email them once. It
shows their jobs, invoices, and any certificates you've issued — so "can you send
that invoice again?" becomes "it's in your link". The link is unique to them;
don't post it anywhere public.

## Keep phone and email filled in

The phone number is how inbox threads, review requests, and reminders find the
right customer — and the email is how their Gmail messages get matched. A
customer with both filled in gets the full experience; one with neither is just
a name on paper.
