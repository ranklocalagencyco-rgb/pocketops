The inbox pulls your customer conversations into one place — SMS to your business
number and emails from your connected Gmail — grouped into threads per customer.

## What lands here

- **Texts** to your business number (set up in **Settings → Messaging**),
  including replies to missed-call text-backs and booking reminders.
- **Emails** from people who are already customers, once Gmail is connected in
  **Settings → Email**. Strangers and spam don't get pulled in.

A badge on the Inbox tab shows unread messages.

## Replying

Type in the thread and send — the app works out the right channel. Text threads
reply by SMS from your business number; email threads reply from your connected
Gmail with the subject kept intact.

Two shortcuts worth knowing:

- **Templates** — your saved messages from **Settings → SMS templates**, one tap
  to insert, with `{name}` filled in automatically.
- **✨ Draft reply** — asks the built-in assistant to draft a reply from the
  conversation so far. Always read it before you send it.

## Missed calls

If someone rings your business number and you can't answer, they automatically
get your missed-call text (edit the wording in **Settings → Messaging**) — and
their reply starts a thread here, so the lead isn't lost.
