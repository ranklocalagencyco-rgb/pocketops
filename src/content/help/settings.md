Settings is where the business gets wired up. Most of it is owner/admin only —
the one everyone gets is **My profile** (your name, mobile, and profile photo).

## Set up once, in this order

1. **Business** — name, address, and logo. This is your letterhead: it appears on
   quotes, invoices, and the customer's payment page.
2. **Regional & VAT** — timezone, currency, VAT rate, and VAT number.
3. **Team** — invite your people. Technicians and apprentices only see jobs
   assigned to them, and never see costs or margins.
4. **Payments** — connect Stripe so customers can pay invoices by card, straight
   into your bank.
5. **Messaging** — your business number, missed-call text-back wording, and
   quiet hours (no automated texts outside the window you set).

## Day-to-day helpers

- **Price Book** — your usual labour, materials, and fees for fast quoting.
- **SMS templates** — the texts you send every day, one tap in the inbox.
- **Documents** — insurance and qualifications, shareable by link and shown as
  "Our credentials" on customer pages.
- **Pipeline insights** — job sources and cancel reasons that feed the dashboard.

## Automation & money

- **Automations** — overdue invoice chasing and recurring service reminders,
  which run each morning (respecting quiet hours).
- **Reviews** — the review link and the text that goes out after payment.
- **Accounting Sync** — connect QuickBooks and paid invoices push across
  automatically.
- **Email** — connect Gmail so customer emails join your inbox threads.
- **Import & export** — bring in customers and job history from other apps;
  export CSVs for your accountant or a full backup any time.
