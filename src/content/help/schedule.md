The schedule is a week-at-a-glance diary of every job with a booked date, shown in
your business's timezone.

## Putting a job on the diary

A job appears here once it reaches the **Scheduled** stage with a date and time
set. You do that from the job's own page — move it to Scheduled and pick the slot.

## Getting around the week

Flick between weeks with the arrows. Each entry shows the customer and job title —
tap through to the full job to see the address, line items, and notes.

## Route today

If you've got several jobs booked for today, the **Route today** button builds a
single Google Maps link with every stop in order — one tap and your sat-nav has
the whole day planned.

## Reminder and "on my way" texts

From a scheduled job you can send the customer a booking reminder or a quick
"on my way" text (needs your business number connected in
**Settings → Messaging**). Automated reminders respect your quiet hours, so
nothing goes out at 6am.
